**After Oil**<br>
*Kuwait Pavilion at the Venice Biennale, Between East and West: a Gulf, 2016*<br>
Curators: Ali Karimi and Hamed Bukhamseen<br>
May 28 - November 27, 2016

After Oil proposes three speculative tales that explore the geography of the Gulf and its islands in the decades after oil. These stories are also a reflection on the present condition: they stage and extrapolate critical issues of today’s oil landscape to make the public aware of the energy systems on which modern life is dependent and the long-term consequences of current fossil fuel regime.

Project Details:<br>
Nine 60x60cm drawings<br>
Three 150X150X15cm models

Project Team:<br>
