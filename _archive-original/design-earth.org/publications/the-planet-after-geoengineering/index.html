The Planet After Geoengineering
Actar, 2021
The graphic novel is a companion to DESIGN EARTH’s eponymous project at the 2021 Venice Biennale of Architecture. The Planet After Geoengineering is a graphic novel in five fictions, each of which imagines the worlds of climate modification technologies and their controversies.
With essays by Kathryn Yusoff, Benjamin Bratton and Holly Jean Buck.
